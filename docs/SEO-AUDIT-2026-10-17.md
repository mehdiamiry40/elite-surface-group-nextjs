Full SEO audit of `elitesurfacegroup.com.au`: the codebase (`src/app`, `src/content`,
`src/lib/seo.ts`, `src/components/JsonLd.tsx`, `next.config.ts`) plus a live
crawl of every sitemap URL.

**Pages analysed:** 41 (every `<loc>` in the live sitemap)

headroom is local trust signals, content depth on commercial pages, and
search-demand coverage, not code.

  canonical, `og:url`, `og:image`, robots, heading tree, `<img>` alt/dimensions,
  JSON-LD types, link counts, word count, inbound contextual links.
- Every internal link target found on those pages requested — **0 broken, 0
  redirecting**.
- Redirect matrix: `http→https`, `www→apex`, missing trailing slash, legacy
  WordPress sitemaps, 404 handling, query-string URLs.
- All 27 distinct Open Graph images requested — all 200.
- Mobile lab Core Web Vitals with Playwright (Moto G4 profile, 4× CPU

## Critical issues (fix first)

None. Nothing found blocks crawling, indexing or ranking.

## High priority (largest ranking upside)

- [ ] **No reviews or testimonials anywhere on the site.** There is no review
  content, no `Review`/`AggregateRating` markup, and no link to a review
  source. For a local trade business, review volume and recency on the Google
  Business Profile is one of the strongest Map Pack factors, and on-site
  testimonials are an E-E-A-T and conversion signal.
  *Fix:* collect reviews on the Google Business Profile; show a few real,
  attributed testimonials on `/`, service pages and case studies. Do **not**
  self-serve `AggregateRating` markup for the business's own reviews (Google
  ignores self-serving review stars for `LocalBusiness`/`Organization`; see the
  earlier finding #14 in `FULL-SCALE-AUDIT.md`).

- [ ] **Google Business Profile is not linked.** `business.social` holds only
  Instagram (`src/content/business.ts:46`), so `sameAs` in the organisation
  schema (`src/app/layout.tsx:33`) has one entry. Add the GBP URL (and any
  real Facebook, LinkedIn, Houzz, hipages or Master Builders SA listings) so
  Google can reconcile the entity. Confirm the GBP name, phone and service
  area exactly match the site (NAP consistency).

- [ ] **Case studies are thin and anonymous.** Project pages run 220–270
  words, describe only what the photo shows, and give no suburb, year, scope,
  substrate, product system, duration or client outcome
  (e.g. `/projects/dark-feature-cladding/`). Five case studies is also a small
  portfolio. These pages are the site's main first-hand-experience evidence.
  *Fix:* add suburb (e.g. "Golden Grove"), completion month, product system,
  problem → approach → result, and a client quote where permitted. Add new
  projects as they finish.

- [ ] **No cost / pricing content.** Nothing on the site mentions prices,
  price ranges or cost drivers. "Render cost per m² Adelaide", "cladding cost
  Adelaide" and "Hebel wall cost" are high-intent queries that competitors
  answer. *Fix:* one cost guide per core service (ranges, what moves the
  price, what a quote includes), linked from each service page.

- [ ] **Only one location page.** `/locations/` has a single child,
  `/locations/adelaide/` (`src/content/locations.ts:31`). The business is based
  in Salisbury East and serves greater Adelaide. *Fix:* add a small number of
  region pages (e.g. Northern suburbs, Adelaide Hills, Eastern suburbs,
  coastal/Western suburbs) **only** where there is real, distinct content —
  local projects, soil/exposure/heritage considerations, council notes.
  Templated suburb-swap pages risk being treated as doorway pages.

## Medium priority

- [ ] **`/blog/` and `/resources/` index the same 20 guides.** Both pages list
  every guide with the same images (24 images each); `/blog/` carries
  excerpts (1,110 words) while `/resources/` is a card grid (395 words). Two
  hubs for one inventory split link equity and can compete for "Adelaide
  render guides"-type queries. `/blog/` has 0 in-content inbound links (only
  header/footer); `/resources/` has 23. *Fix:* pick one canonical hub — keep
  `/resources/` (all guide URLs live under it) and 301 `/blog/` to it, or
  differentiate `/blog/` into a chronological feed with only recent posts.

- [ ] **Newer guides are under-linked.** Contextual inbound links from other
  pages: `render-cracking` 19, but `vertical-vs-horizontal-cladding`,
  `asbestos-cladding-replacement`, `soundproofing-walls`,
  `render-finishes`, `external-wall-insulation` only 3 each. *Fix:* link each
  guide from its parent service page's "related guides" block and from 2–3
  sibling guides.

- [ ] **Organisation / LocalBusiness schema is missing recommended fields**
  (`src/app/layout.tsx:74`): no `priceRange`, no `geo`, and `image` is the
  192 px logo rather than a representative project photo. Add
  `priceRange` (e.g. `"$$"`), `geo` for Salisbury East at suburb precision,
  and an `image` array with 1–3 project photos.

- [ ] **Guides are authored by the organisation only.** `ArticleSchema`
  (`src/components/JsonLd.tsx:155`) sets `author` to the Organization and
  pages show no named expert. For YMYL-adjacent topics (asbestos,
  load-bearing walls, salt damp) a named, credentialed author with a short
  bio (licence class, years in trade) strengthens E-E-A-T. Add a `Person`
  author with `jobTitle` and `worksFor`.

- [ ] **Business email is a Gmail address** (`elite.surfacegroup@gmail.com`).
  A domain address (`info@elitesurfacegroup.com.au`) is a small but real
  trust/legitimacy signal and keeps NAP consistent with the website domain.

- [ ] **Home page CLS is close to the threshold** — 0.096 in the throttled
  mobile run (limit 0.1); other pages 0.017–0.059. Worth confirming in Search
  Console's Core Web Vitals report once field data exists; likely sources are
  the hero carousel or the font swap.

## Low priority / opportunities

- [ ] **Home title leads with the brand.** `Elite Surface Group | Adelaide
  Cladding & Render` — consider `Cladding & Render Adelaide | Elite Surface
  Group` so the query terms come first. Also add "Hebel" if it fits.

- [ ] **Some titles exceed ~60 characters** and will truncate:
  `/services/` (64), `rendering-over-painted-brick` (65),
  `acrylic-render-vs-cement-render` (65), `steel-frame-vs-timber-frame` (64),
  `salt-damp` (63). The ` — Elite Surface Group` suffix costs 22 characters;
  shorten the page part or drop the suffix on long guide titles.

- [ ] **Short meta descriptions** on `/contact-us/` (94), `/privacy-policy/`
  (87), `/terms-of-service/` (92) and two case studies (106–109). Low impact,
  easy to extend toward 140–155.

- [ ] **Keyword-light H1s** on `/about/` ("The Team Behind the Finish"),
  `/contact-us/` ("Let's Talk About Your Project") and `/locations/` ("Where We
  Work"). Fine for brand voice; consider adding "Adelaide" or the service set.

- [ ] **Two-hop redirect on `www` URLs without a trailing slash.**
  `www…/cladding` → `www…/cladding/` → `https://elitesurfacegroup.com.au/cladding/`.
  Also `www…/sitemap.xml` → `/sitemap.xml/` → `/sitemap.xml`. Harmless for
  ranking, but a single hop is cleaner; make the host redirect in
  `next.config.ts:97` fire first or set the apex redirect at the Vercel domain
  level.

- [ ] **FAQPage markup no longer produces rich results** for commercial sites
  (Google limited FAQ rich results to authoritative government/health sites
  in 2023). Keep it — it is valid and still useful to AI answer engines — but
  don't expect SERP FAQ dropdowns.

- [ ] **`llms.txt` omits `/locations/`** (and legal pages, which is fine).
  Add the locations hub, and add each new guide/case study as it ships.

- [ ] **Search Console verification not visible in code.** No
  `google-site-verification` meta tag; if verification is via DNS that's fine.
  Confirm the property exists, the sitemap is submitted, and check the Page
  indexing report for any of the 41 URLs marked "Crawled – currently not
  indexed".

- [ ] **Web Analytics data unavailable.** The Vercel API reports Web
  Analytics as not found for `elite-surface-group-nextjs`. Check it is
  enabled in the Vercel dashboard (Project → Analytics); without it there is
  no organic-traffic or landing-page data to measure this work against.

- [ ] **Body copy renders at 14 px on mobile.** Readable, but 16 px is the
  usual baseline for long-form guides.

## Passing — what's done well

- **Sitemap:** 41 URLs, all 200, apex HTTPS, trailing slashes, editorial
  `lastmod` dates (not build time) derived from content
  (`src/content/routes.ts`). Legacy WordPress sitemap URLs 308 to
  `/sitemap.xml`.
- **robots.txt:** allows everything except `/api/`, points at the sitemap,
  explicitly welcomes AI search crawlers.
- **Canonicals and `og:url`:** present and self-referencing on all 41 pages;
  query strings (`?utm_source=`) canonicalise correctly.
- **Titles and descriptions:** unique on every page — no duplicates.
- **Social cards:** 27 purpose-built 1200×630 OG images, all resolving; Twitter
  `summary_large_image` everywhere.
- **Headings:** exactly one H1 per page, no skipped levels, no empty headings.
- **Images:** `next/image` with AVIF/WebP, explicit dimensions, blur
  placeholders, `fetchpriority="high"` preload for the hero; meaningful alt
  text on content images, empty alt only on decorative banners/logo.
- **Structured data:** `HomeAndConstructionBusiness` + `WebSite` sitewide;
  `Service` + `FAQPage` on service pages; `Article` with published/modified
  dates on guides; `CreativeWork` on case studies; `BreadcrumbList` and
  `ItemList` on hubs. All JSON-LD parses.
- **Redirects:** `http→https` and `www→apex` are single 308s for slash-ended
  URLs; unknown paths return real 404s.
- **Performance:** server-rendered, ~160 KB JS, ~360–440 KB total transfer;
  mobile lab LCP 0.85–1.7 s.
- **Security headers:** HSTS preload, CSP, `nosniff`, frame denial — all
  present.
- **Content:** 20 in-depth, Adelaide-specific guides of 1,400–2,400 words
  answering real homeowner questions, each linked to a service.
- **AI visibility:** `llms.txt` published and current for all guides.
